What is preorder traversal?
 - In a binary tree, preorder traversal follows this order:
 - Root → Left → Right


Steps
 1- Visit the current node (Root) → do something with it (like print)
 2- Traverse the left subtree (recursively)
 3- Traverse the right subtree (recursively)



 Example:
      10
     /  \
    5    15
   / \   / \
  2   7 12 20


 - output: 10 → 5 → 2 → 7 → 15 → 12 → 20
 - Notice: Root first, then left, then right




When is preorder useful?
 - Copying a tree → you visit root first, then create subtrees
 - Expression trees → gives prefix notation
 - Save/serialize a tree → preorder allows reconstruction
