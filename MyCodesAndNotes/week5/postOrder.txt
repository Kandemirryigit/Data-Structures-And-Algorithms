What is postorder traversal?
 - In a binary tree, postorder traversal follows this order:
 - Left → Right → Root


Steps
 1- Traverse the left subtree (recursively)
 2- Traverse the right subtree (recursively)
 3- Visit the current node (Root) → do something with it (like print)



Example: 

      50
     /  \
    30   70
   / \
  20  40

  - Output: 20 → 40 → 30 → 70 → 50
  - Notice: Root comes last, after all children



When is postorder useful?
 - Deleting a tree
   - You need to delete children before deleting root
 
 - Expression trees
   - Produces postfix notation (useful in calculators/compilers)
 
 - Bottom-up calculations
   - Any problem where you need results from children first
