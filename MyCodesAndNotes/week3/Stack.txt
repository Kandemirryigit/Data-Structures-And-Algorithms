What is a Stack?
 - Imagine a pile of plates in a cafeteria
 - You can only add a new plate on the top
 - You can only take a plate from the top
 - You can never take a plate from the middle or bottom.
 - That's exactly how a stack works.
 - Last In, First Out — LIFO
 - The last thing you put in is the first thing that comes out.




Real World Examples of Stacks
 - Undo button in any app
    - every action you do gets pushed onto a stack
    - When you press undo, the last action gets popped off.

 - Browser back button
    - every page you visit gets pushed. 
    - When you press back, the last page gets popped.

 


The Three Operations
 - Push — add an element to the top. O(1)
 - Pop — remove the element from the top. O(1)
 - Peek — look at the top element without removing it. O(1)
 - All three are always O(1). That's the beauty of a stack.
