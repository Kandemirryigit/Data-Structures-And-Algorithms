What is a Queue?
 - Imagine you are at a McDonald's line.
 - The first person who joins the line gets served first
 - New people join from the back of the line
 - People leave from the front of the line
 - Nobody can cut in the middle
 - That is exactly a queue.




The Golden Rule — FIFO
 - First In First Out
    - The first element you put in is the first element you get out
    - Completely opposite of a stack.

    - Stack  →  LIFO  →  last in first out  →  pile of plates
    - Queue  →  FIFO  →  first in first out →  line at McDonald's
